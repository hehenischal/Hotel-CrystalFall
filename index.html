CrystalFall Hotel

Located on Street 2 of Lakeside, Pokhara, Crystal Fall Hotel offers a serene escape in the heart of Nepal’s most picturesque city. Surrounded by breathtaking views of Phewa Lake and the majestic Annapurna range, the Hotel combines modern luxury with the charm of nature. Enjoy world-class comfort, exceptional service, and an unforgettable experience where every moment feels like a getaway.
